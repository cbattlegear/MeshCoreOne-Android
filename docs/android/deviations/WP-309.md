# WP-309: reactions, actions, and message details

Reference: `db14559b39d32322b06477c6ae676112f583db50`. Integrated base:
`f34f68d0416da113c2cd0d274c44937f6c41a5d3` (`origin/main`). Owner:
`chats-ui-engineer`.

## Native adaptations

- **Channel sender DM matching (#722).** Like Swift's `SendDMSheet`, the mounted channel action
  now presents all radio-scoped, case-insensitive exact name matches, excluding blocked contacts,
  and requires explicit selection even for one match. It shares the room picker surface and keeps
  the name-only limitation and raw sender name beside any unverified nickname. Unlike Swift's
  logged fetch failure followed by empty content, Android shows the failure with retry. Selection
  rechecks the stored contact, original channel occupant/session and originating navigation entry;
  cancel or stale ownership cannot navigate. `ChannelSenderDirectMessageConsumerTest` covers the
  mounted timeline/action/picker/navigation flow, empty/one/multiple matches, case variants, blocked
  and foreign-radio contacts, nickname disclosure, cancel, read recovery and stale ownership.
  The shared Material sheet is scrollable, fully expanded and has 48 dp targets rather than iOS
  medium/large detents; compact and expanded/200% font cases use the existing Robolectric harness.

### Issue #729: message and sender text rules

This feature explicitly uses upstream revision
`5e980a53e077c45a1270170612fdf5dae97792ff` (`TextRulesPreference`, channel/DM/room admission,
recently-blocked storage/ownership and Settings). These files are absent from the bundled reference;
the reference pin and its inventory are unchanged.

- Installation-wide rules and type selections use a versioned Android-local Room singleton instead of
  the upstream atomic JSON file. Conversation incarnation/override rows and the bounded 100-message
  archive share Room transactions with admission; deletion triggers retire identities and owned
  contact/room archives even when the writer is a backup or another repository. Channel deletion
  retains old archive entries, as upstream does. Existing chat text is not rewritten.
  Settings operate offline, independently of connection services. Conversation IDs are not exported;
  restoring a deleted contact cannot inherit its old fixed selection or override.
  Rule-selection incarnations do not stamp RF-origin proof onto incoming messages.
  An unresolved channel witness retains incoming messages without origin proof or scoped filtering;
  Android cannot attribute those frames to a conversation incarnation until reconciliation. A
  positively stale witness still rejects admission. Demoting a device to a ghost preserves the
  surviving radio's archive, overrides and fixed selections.
  Contact/room lookups capture their local conversation incarnation in the same Room transaction.
  Admission rejects snapshots predating deletion/restoration, even if the UUID and public key are
  reused; replacement history, rules, unread counts, activity and connection state remain untouched.
  These receive-only witnesses are excluded from source-value equality, database owner rows and backup
  v1. `TextRulePersistenceTest` exercises both filtered and unfiltered replacement conversations.
- Incoming DM and room replay admission verifies the full persisted wire identity and exact text in
  addition to the upstream truncated content key. Backup restoration uses the same distinguishing
  fields alongside that key, so hash collisions, different sender prefixes, text types or room authors
  remain distinct and child reactions/repeats stay with the correct message. A DM missing both a sender
  and contact or its original corrected timestamp uses its UUID rather than merging unproved identities.
  This avoids irreversible history loss without changing the backup format or the source key.
  `IncomingTextRulesTest` and `BackupIdentityIntegrationTest` cover collision, replay, restore and
  merge behavior.
- Android commits a newly admitted channel or direct message and its applicable activity/unread
  bookkeeping together. A failed Room write retains the polled frame for retry, rather than saving
  history that would later be mistaken for an already-bookkept replay. A channel witness that becomes
  unresolved or changes secret does not apply its captured rule or update/notify that slot; its unproved
  message is retained. The channel is checked again before notification if it changes after admission.
  `IncomingTextRulesTest` covers rollback, retry, active chats and witness changes.
- Unlike upstream's separate persistence and publication steps, Android retains the committed direct
  or room admission for the poller's original delivery while post-commit reads are retried. A bounded
  commit callback preserves the saved/blocked/recovered decision even when cancellation arrives after
  Room commits; cancellation still propagates. Room publication rechecks the captured conversation
  incarnation and saved message, so a deleted and restored room cannot receive stale events or
  notifications. `IncomingTextRulesTest` covers read failure, cancellation, replay and replacement.
- When a receive clock cannot fit UInt32, Android reports the failed last-heard stamp but still commits
  the message and its applicable activity/unread state, retaining the original wire timestamp. This
  preserves `TimestampRangeRecoveryTest`; `TextRulePersistenceTest` also covers reactions and both
  clock boundaries without suppressing unrelated storage errors.
- Native Material controls under Settings > Chats preserve rule order, literal case-insensitive
  contains/whole-field matching, significant whitespace, per-type off/all/fixed selection and per-chat
  inherit/on/off. The actual Settings destination, rule drafts and archive selection survive saved-state
  restoration. Original sender/body fields split only on standalone delimiter/framing-space graphemes.
  Strings use Android resources in all 12 supported languages, reusing the explicit upstream revision's labels where
  applicable; the frozen Swift localization catalog is untouched.
  Regex quoting prevents patterns from becoming regular expressions; Unicode-aware matching retains
  UTF-16 offsets and expands display highlights to grapheme boundaries.
- Corrupt preference bytes remain intact, editing fails, and receive continues without text filtering
  with an explicit load-failure signal, matching upstream. Archive-write failures roll back admission
  and propagate the repository's typed reported failure to the poller, which retains the frame for
  storage recovery; they never become a successful suppression.
  Archive review remains available if preference decoding alone fails. Retry restarts a failed archive
  observer as well as reloading settings; `TextRuleSettingsComposeTest` covers later archive updates.
- `TextRulesTest`, `TextRulePersistenceTest`, `BackupTextRulesTest`, `IncomingTextRulesTest`, and
  the Room migration/Compose settings tests assert the native behavior, including failed saves and
  preserved drafts, archive review during preference corruption, and 200% font/saved-state choices.
  Android assertions remain unverified until Android CI executes them. No device, TalkBack, hardware
  or iOS backup interchange result is implied.

- **Path detail read failure.** Android requires a data source rather than Swift's nullable store.
  A failed contacts, discovered-node, or repeat read clears the path directory and presents a
  retryable error instead of displaying incomplete details as a successful load.
- **D-1 Compose action surface.** Swift menus and sheets are represented by a retained state holder and an
  adaptive Compose content surface. Compact layouts show one scrollable detail pane; widths of 600 dp or
  more place the selected path map beside the details. Action execution stays callback-driven so the
  existing timeline/composer owners retain reply, copy, resend, reaction, and delete behavior.
  The successor removes the active Translate button under the approved translation scope removal;
  the inert enum remains for consumer compatibility, not as a reachable action or deferred feature.
- **D-2 Shared-map rendering.** Path details consume WP-312's `MapLibreMapSurface`, OpenFreeMap catalog,
  marker/line algebra, camera calculation, and visible provider attribution. WP-309 adds no provider,
  account, key, Google service, location permission, offline claim, or alternate map behavior.
- **D-3 Durable-data boundary.** `MessageActionsDataSource` is the feature-owned adapter for process
  contacts/discovered nodes, repeat/reaction records, and block/mute writes. The state holder never owns a
  radio session. Feature-local name confidence, reaction-summary, and SNR presentation types avoid a
  forbidden concrete `feature:chats` to `core:services` edge. Cancellation propagates; read, block, and
  mute failures remain typed UI state rather than becoming empty success.
- **D-4 Path resolution.** Stable endpoint/hop IDs are derived from identity bytes, not list positions.
  Ambiguous short hashes remain fallback matches and cannot be drawn as exact located hops. Duplicate
  located hops are rendered once while preserving the original arrival order and completeness result.
- **D-5 Delivery accessibility.** Delivery state is exposed as a textual enum and Compose labels include
  status, SNR quality/value, repeats, path availability, and selectable reaction counts. Color remains
  supplementary. Deterministic JVM tests cover these contracts; physical TalkBack, screenshots, and device
  rendering are not claimed.
- **D-6 Preview adaptation.** Shared-map camera/canvas state replaces Swift snapshot image storage inside
  this feature. Width bucketing and stable preview keys are preserved, while snapshot download/cache
  ownership stays with WP-312. No network-backed preview success is manufactured.

## Deliberate boundaries

The manifest owns the `actions/` implementation and test packages. Existing WP-307 timeline and WP-310
room/channel files are preserved unchanged; they consume the public action/state callbacks when assembled
by their screen owner. No feature-to-feature dependency, second persistence store, radio lifecycle, new
permission, or replacement MapLibre/OpenFreeMap path was introduced.

- **D-13 Selected-arrival path copy.** Frozen `MessagePathContent` shows the arrow-separated path and

- **D-14 Road-distance banner and incomplete disclosure.** Frozen preview/banner displays abbreviated

## Fetched-history failure and cancellation successor

Base: `fd3efe2be3f1837d39636433cc82fae80f4294ae`; frozen source unchanged.
This directly coupled pre-existing failure path was not introduced by the
older-page overlap change.

- **D-15 Failed replay is not acknowledgement.** The pinned Swift helper and
  prior Android App consumer both drain matching pending entries before store
  access. Neither restores a failed batch; the duplicate guard also skips a
  summary repair after a partial commit. Android now retains the uncompleted
  portion of each drained batch in the same service queue on failure or
  cancellation, preserving original parsed data, scope, raw text and `receivedAt`.
  Two narrow service entry points restore exact objects through the existing
  bounded queue; hash/parser/cache selection and connection lifetime are unchanged.
- Nullable service persistence failure now becomes an explicit typed history-load
  failure after the existing service logs the underlying cause. Cancellation
  propagates and requeues synchronously; it is not shown as a persistence failure.
  An already-saved row is not recreated: the App consumer rebuilds and persists
  its summary with the existing parser summary helper before acknowledging it.
  Retry is the next explicit history open/refresh/loadOlder operation, not a new
  background scheduler or a durable cross-session pending queue.
- This is a deliberate failure-recovery improvement over the frozen helper's
  loss-on-failure behavior, not a claim that the Swift reference already retries.
  Success, reaction identity, actual store scope, selective sender skipping,
  queue bounds and successful timeline reconciliation remain preserved.
  See [failure-schedule evidence](../evidence/WP-309/history-reaction-replay-failures.md)
  for actual successor verification; earlier Kotlin passes do not verify it.

- **D-16 Shared persistence/summary boundary.** Duplicate-history reconciliation
  and normal live persistence can otherwise interleave across store suspensions:
  a captured older reaction list can overwrite a newer stored summary, then
  history acknowledges its pending entry. One process-factory-owned mutex spans
  save/fetch/summary for normal persistence and fetch/summary for duplicate retry.
  The private locked helper does not reacquire that non-reentrant mutex. Pending
  queue/cache locks remain synchronous and independent; no store callback needs
  the persistence mutex. Cancellation while waiting or writing still propagates.
  This is explicit native full-operation serialization, not an assertion that
  Swift actor isolation is atomic across suspension. Six additional App/Room
  controls cover both operation orders and cancellation of a waiting replay in
  both scopes, using the actual live-sync persistence adapter and explicit gates.
  The original 30 failure/cancellation/metadata controls remain unchanged.
  A reconnect successor shares this boundary across replacement service instances:
  screen-owned history can still hold the old service after teardown. Only the
  persistence mutex survives connection replacement; pending queues, cache and
  radio services remain per generation. Six further App/Room controls cross real
  same-radio disconnect/connect with old history held at a store suspension,
  exercising both operation orders and cancellation of an old waiting replay.

- **D-17 Existence is rechecked inside the operation.** App history and live sync
  retain their early duplicate checks, but those reads can both observe absence
  before either writer acquires the shared mutex. The store's UUID upsert does
  not enforce semantic uniqueness. The service now reuses the actual store's
  `(radio, message, sender, emoji)` matcher under the same mutex before saving.
  An existing row keeps its original identity/metadata and still gets summary
  reconciliation. A failed final check is logged and fails the operation;
  cancellation propagates. No stored rows are deleted or rewritten to deduplicate
  historical data, and no origin/occupant proof is inferred.
  Eight real Room/polling-handler schedules synchronize both outside checks at
  false before either insert, in both scopes/orders and across reconnect.
  Two distinct-emoji controls complement the retained distinct-sender cases.
  Four final-check failure/cancellation controls preserve unfinished batches;
  the original outer-check controls still interrupt Carol after Bob completed.

- **D-18 Restoration retains eviction priority.** Restoring a drained key through
  a normal append incorrectly makes it newest: a failed oldest-key replay at
  capacity can evict newer, unrelated pending reactions. The bounded queue now
  assigns monotonic key/entry ordinals and returns them with immutable pending
  values. Restoration merges by those ordinals rather than `receivedAt`, including
  a new arrival under the drained key before restoration. Entries are still
  evicted oldest-entry-of-oldest-key, with the same 100-entry bound and no TTL.
  Empty/drained keys retain no queue tombstones; ordering metadata travels only
  with returned values. Clear removes all queued values and keys but retains the
  ordinal counter so an older in-flight value cannot collide with a new arrival.
  No timestamp, parser, matcher, persistence or connection-factory behavior changes.
  Eight real App/Room controls cover failure/cancellation in DM/channel with
  multiple full-queue keys, both with and without a same-key arrival while replay
  is suspended. All prior 65 history/failure/concurrency methods remain.

- **D-19 Collision- and incarnation-safe text admission.** The pinned Swift
  receive path treats its short content keys as definitive duplicates and
  separately updates the captured contact. Android verifies a direct sender
  prefix, original timestamp, persisted text type and exact body, and verifies
  the room author, timestamp and exact body after a key match. Direct admission
  commits the message and current contact's heard/activity/unread state together,
  and rechecks the contact incarnation and message before publication. This
  prevents hash collisions from discarding different text and prevents a stale
  snapshot from updating or notifying for a blocked or replaced contact. Unknown
  wire text types retain the existing plain-text clamping policy. The focused
  `IncomingTextRulesTest` collision, blocking, replacement and rollback cases
  exercise the real polling and Room paths.
