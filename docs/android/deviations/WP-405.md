# WP-405 deviations

## Current-main channel confirmation and allocation

The Swift confirmation flow maps to a shell-owned Material dialog rather than
one dialog per composed navigation entry. Channel links and hashtags have
separate delivery UUIDs and form identities: an old completion can acknowledge
only its own request, and the holder survives a chat transition without replay.

Confirmed allocation re-reads authoritative occupied slots inside the same
persisted channel operation and session dispatch-fence scope as SET/clear and
channel synchronization. It uses that operation's scoped store, never a nested
public mutation. Unknown slots require a real radio read before allocation;
uncertain dispatched writes require reconnection/reconciliation, not another
allocation based on cached rows. A cached existing hashtag remains a read-only
offline navigation path.

The UUID actually returned by saveChannel is acknowledged outside operation,
identity and cache lanes even if subsequent fetch/cache work fails or is
cancelled. Reusing an existing hashtag produces no write receipt. A receipt
observer failure cannot replace the primary failure/cancellation. These are
native concurrency adaptations, covered by ChannelServiceNativeTest,
ChannelConfirmationConsumerTest and ChannelSheetsStateHolderTest.

The November reconciliation preserves main's merged contact, lifecycle and
navigation-store implementations and composes the scoped service lane from the
WP-202/WP-209 successor. ADR-007 supersedes the historical process descriptions
below; no reservation, execution lock, approval gate or CI-result bundle is
introduced.

## WP405-LINKS repair boundary

This local batch addresses retained APP-G03, APP-G04 and APP-G07 only. It does not
change graph/contact repairs, cancellation policy, composer continuity or release
behavior. Source pin and original message text/localization remain unchanged.

The regression additions exercise the real retained navigation ViewModel with
`ACTION_VIEW`, App routing and the navigation coordinator; the original San
Francisco focus must be held until binding, routed immediately after binding,
and never replayed after consumption. The mounted map-thumbnail regression
checks both the retained San Francisco and London destination coordinates rather
than merely the selected tab, after actual initial sync completion.
The mounted conversation regression uses the production message-body mention
producer and custom-action dispatcher through the contact destination, including
literal percent escapes, percent signs, slashes, spaces and Unicode.

Channel confirmation regressions use the mounted App dialog, a protocol-speaking
fake radio and Room, asserting no write before consent and exact secret/scope
after consent. Region regressions cover external/chat routing and the QR
parser-to-join seam through the real App channel service and local preference
write. The QR seam is not a camera or physical-device observation.

The complete supported affected run passed at `35c1064d5`: App 1,571 and chats
392 discovered/passed cases, zero failures/errors/skips, including the named
mounted/Room/producer regressions. This is local automated evidence, not physical
device, camera, hardware or human acceptance. See the repair evidence section
for exact bindings, command, outcomes and the corrected initial compile failure.
The chat/QR normalizer was admitted after trusted-main support landing
`f2f481d2e7c2a9029808943529c98c98ef879299`, using capability
`wp405-uri-region-normalization`, operation `repair-uri-region-normalization`,
and same-owner reservation revision 4. It reuses the unchanged protocol helper
and shared limit without changing URI validation, decoding or secret behavior.

No frozen Swift input was changed or advanced beyond
`db14559b39d32322b06477c6ae676112f583db50`. No permission, billing,
analytics, updater, evidence-retention, reservation, or approval machinery was added.
